How to read the drawing

Each point is one element of the poset. Its coordinates are its ranks in two linear extensions whose intersection is the poset. The rank sequence below the plot lists the vertical coordinates from left to right. Each frame is one isomorphism class, not every possible drawing of that class.

How the representatives were selected

The generator and verification code adds a maximal element above each order ideal of every smaller poset, canonicalizes the resulting directed comparability graph to remove isomorphic copies, and rejects posets with a nontrivial module. It finds two linear extensions that order every incomparable pair oppositely, then verifies that the resulting coordinate order recovers every comparison. The counts for sizes 4 through 9 are 1, 4, 25, 174, 1481, and 14136.

The seven-element row is useful to inspect: the 1994 count of two-dimensional posets gives 174 prime cases, while the displayed Table 1 in the 1998 paper gives 124. The generator here produces 174 distinct representatives; the cause of the displayed discrepancy is unclear.
